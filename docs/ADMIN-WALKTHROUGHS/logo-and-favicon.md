# Walkthrough — Logo (Header + Footer) & Favicon

## Change the logo (shows in header and footer)

1. In WordPress admin, go to `Appearance → Customize → Site Identity`.
2. Under **Logo**, click **Select logo** (or **Change logo**).
3. Upload/select the image and confirm.
4. Click **Publish**.

### Verify

- Open the site homepage.
- Confirm the logo appears:
  - In the **header** (top of page)
  - In the **footer** (bottom of page)

## Change the favicon (Site Icon)

1. Go to `Appearance → Customize → Site Identity`.
2. Under **Site Icon**, click **Select site icon**.
3. Upload/select a square image (WordPress will crop).
4. Click **Publish**.

### Verify

- Open the site in a browser tab and confirm the icon appears in the tab.
